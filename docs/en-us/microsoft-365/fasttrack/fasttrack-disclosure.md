# FastTrack Disclosure \(v2.0\)

## FastTrack additional privacy disclosure

This additional privacy disclosure explains what data Microsoft collects through your use of FastTrack Services and how we use that data. This additional privacy disclosure supplements the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Overview

FastTrack Services are an ongoing and repeatable service benefit to help customers realize business value faster with the Microsoft Cloud \("FastTrack Services"\). FastTrack Services help to:

- Migrate email or other content, and light up Microsoft 365 services.
- Deploy and help securely manage devices.
- Enable your business and gain end-user adoption.
- Plan, onboard, and drive adoption and usage.
- Move to the cloud confidently at your own pace.

FastTrack Services are available to qualifying customers, and delivered by Microsoft employees, Microsoft-approved suppliers, and Microsoft-approved partners \("FastTrack Specialists"\). FastTrack Services are "Professional Services", subject to the Professional Services Notice in the [Online Service Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs) \("OST"\).

Some FastTrack Services may consist of performing configuration tasks \(where applicable and feasible\) or other services described in [Data Migration](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/fasttrack/data-migration) \(collectively, "Data Migration Services"\).

## Data collection and use

### Administrator data

We collect certain Administrator Data \(as defined in the [Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)\) in order to implement your onboarding, migration plan, or fulfill other requests for assistance. Administrator Data may include: \(i\) your name, title, email address, and phone number, or \(ii\) the names, titles, email addresses, and phone numbers of individuals who you \(or your organization\) elect to share with us in order to facilitate the FastTrack Services. We use Administrator Data to provide the FastTrack Services and will disclose Administrator Data to your designated FastTrack Specialists. FastTrack Specialists may only access and use your Administrator Data in accordance with our data privacy and security requirements and may not access or use such data for any other purpose. We will retain the Administrator Data of your tenant admin in accordance with the terms of the online service\(s\) or other Microsoft services associated with your account. For Administrator Data of individuals that you \(or your organization\) elect\(s\) to share with Microsoft for purposes of facilitating FastTrack Services, you \(not Microsoft\) are responsible for updating and deleting such data, through your tenant admin. For clarity, such responsibilities will include responding to any requests from individuals pursuant to the European Union General Data Protection Regulation \(GDPR\), including individuals' rights to access, retrieve, correct, erase, and restrict processing of their Personal Data. Under GDPR, Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

### Migration data

Once we commence the Data Migration Services, data you select for migration from your current systems to the online services is considered "Migration Data" during the performance of FastTrack Services. As selected by you, Migration Data could include Customer Data \(including personal data contained therein\). Only after FastTrack Services are successfully completed, and Migration Data has been transferred into the destination online services is such data Customer Data for purposes of the OST. For further information on how we use Customer Data in our Enterprise Online Services, please consult the [Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) and [Online Service Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs).

During the performance of FastTrack Services, Microsoft may transfer Migration Data to the United States or any other country in which Microsoft or its affiliates or subcontractors maintain facilities. The legal framework for such transfers of data is governed by the provision in the OST entitled "Location of data processing".

You may choose to disengage from the FastTrack Services or terminate a migration any time. We will delete Migration Data \(or any copies thereof\) within 30 days of the conclusion or termination of any Migration Services.

You should retain a copy of Migration Data in your existing systems for your ordinary use until Data Migration Services are complete, including meeting your legal or privacy requirements, and not delete such copy\(ies\) until you have confirmed successful completion of Data Migration Services. Please note that some Migration Services inherently delete all data in accordance with requirements under applicable law, and in such cases you will therefore need to fulfill any individual's requests under GDPR that pertain to such Migration Data, based on the source data. For all other Migration Services, if during the Migration Services you receive a request from an individual to delete his or her personal data contained in the Migration Data, your tenant admin will submit the request to Microsoft on behalf of such individual \(the tenant admin will be made aware of how to submit such requests\).

## Data disclosure obligations

In some cases, and in Microsoft's sole discretion, Microsoft may share certain personal data regarding its employees, contractors, or suppliers with you to comply with your security obligations for FastTrack Services. Such information will be used solely for the limited security obligations it was disclosed for and will remain Microsoft's confidential information. You further agree to delete all such personal data within 30 days of the conclusion of the FastTrack Services associated with the disclosure.

## Security practices in FastTrack

This section outlines security practices employed in FastTrack to help protect data from threats and help ensure that unauthorized access and data incidents can be detected and contained in a timely manner.

### Protect

- Data protection requirements are applied in the organization to help minimize and secure access to sensitive data.
- Multifactor authentication is employed to help control identity and access management.
- Software-defined networks help isolate infrastructure partitions and protect our cloud infrastructure from intrusions and distributed denial of service attacks.
- Security hygiene is maintained through up-to-date, anti-malware software and adherence to strict vulnerability management policy.
- Security awareness is maintained through mandatory security and privacy trainings and webinars.

### Detect

- Monitoring network environments 24x7 for potential cybersecurity events.
- Security assessment and testing of operations and infrastructure for detecting potential threats.

### Respond

- Well-defined response processes help to contain incidents.
- A multidisciplinary team specializes in incident response, forensics, intrusion analysis, and deep understanding of the platforms, services, and applications operating in our cloud datacenters.
- In the unlikely event an incident should occur, there is a well-defined and tested process to notify customers appropriately.

## FastTrack subprocessor list

This Microsoft FastTrack subprocessor list identifies the subprocessors authorized to access Migration Data, in order to provide FastTrack Services. Any such subprocessors \(including their subsidiaries\) will be permitted to process Migration Data only to deliver the services Microsoft has retained them to provide, and they are prohibited from using Migration Data for any other purpose.

Microsoft publishes the names of new subprocessors for FastTrack Services at least six months in advance of their authorization to perform services that may involve access to Migration Data. Subprocessors provide migration or onboarding services, including ancillary services or contract staff in support of FastTrack Services.

- Subprocessors who provide ancillary services may process, store, or otherwise access limited Customer Data, including personal data contained therein, in the course of providing their services.
- Subprocessor contract staff work in close coordination with Microsoft employees to help support, operate, and maintain the FastTrack Services and in the course of doing so may be exposed to Customer Data \(including personal data contained therein\). In such cases, Customer Data still resides only in Microsoft facilities, on Microsoft systems, and subject to Microsoft policies and supervision.

All FastTrack subprocessors are required to maintain the confidentiality of this data and are contractually obligated to meet strict privacy requirements that are equivalent to or stronger than the contractual commitments Microsoft makes to its customers.
