# FastTrack for Microsoft 365 Benefit

## What is FastTrack for Microsoft 365?

FastTrack offers a range of services and resources to help organizations learn, prepare and deploy Copilot, Security, and Microsoft 365 including assistance for data migration.

From digital based guidance, working directly with FastTrack Engineering, or with partners on engagements, FastTrack adapts to an organization’s complexity and readiness. FastTrack guides are available to everyone via Copilot in the Microsoft Admin Center or on the web. As a Microsoft engineering backed service, FastTrack helps accelerate onboarding, getting customers up and running faster while avoiding common pitfalls with proven best practices.

FastTrack Ready Partners represents Microsoft’s premier deployment and adoption partners. Backed by engineering, these partners can ensure customers are aware of the latest guides and resources, while offering their services for customized needs.

## Get started with FastTrack

Ask your Microsoft Account Representative for details or follow these steps to submit a Request for Assistance \(RFA\).

1. Visit the [Request for Assistance](https://setup.cloud.microsoft/microsoft-365-fasttrack-assistance?Q=LearnFTBenefit) page and **Sign in**.

   Note

   Note that if you're a tenant admin, you are automatically taken to the Microsoft 365 admin center.
2. Select **Request assistance**.
3. Select the Microsoft 365 Product you would like deployment assistance with.
4. Select **Edit** to update the deployment and contact details, and click **Submit** to create the request.

Ready to get started? This video guides you through submitting a request and explains next steps.

Note

If deployment guidance for a product is not listed in the FastTrack service, complete the Request for Assistance form, to ensure you’re directed to the most appropriate resources for your deployment goals and organizational needs. Once submitted, your request is reviewed and routed to a resource who can best support your deployment goals.

Note

*Please note that the scope and SLA of support may vary depending on the specific workload. FastTrack can help recommend resources from self-guidance, Microsoft Unified offerings, or Microsoft partners to meet your deployment needs.*

Learn more about FastTrack:
