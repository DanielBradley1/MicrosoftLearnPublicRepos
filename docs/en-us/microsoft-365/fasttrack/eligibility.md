# Eligibility

FastTrack deployment guidance and assistance is available for customer tenants with 150 or more licenses for Copilot, Security, Migrations, and most workloads in Microsoft 365. These plans can be for an individual product \(*like Microsoft Copilot*\) or a suite of products \(*like Microsoft 365 E3*\). Because Microsoft provides many purchasing options, the list of eligible plans is extensive and is found in [Eligible plans](#eligible-plans).
