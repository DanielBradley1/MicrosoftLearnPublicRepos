# Appendix A - FastTrack HIPAA Business Associate Agreement

If you have a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement \(BAA\) agreed with Microsoft, all services listed in [Office 365](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/fasttrack/office-365) are performed in accordance with that BAA.
