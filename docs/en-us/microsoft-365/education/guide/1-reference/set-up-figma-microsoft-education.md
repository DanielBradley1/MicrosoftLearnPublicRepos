# Set up Figma for Microsoft Education

Have questions? Email [education@figma.com](mailto:education@figma.com) directly.

Figma is the leading web-based, collaborative creativity tool that allows your education organization to create, share, and ideate together in real time. Two free products are available for schools:

- **Figma**, the industry-standard design platform, is used to make wireframes, software prototypes, and so much more.
- **FigJam**, our online whiteboard, facilitates collaboration for any class activity like brainstorming, ice breakers, or making learning visual.

Qualified K12 organizations are granted access to Figma's [Enterprise](https://www.figma.com/pricing/) license plan. Enabling Figma's Enterprise plan for your organization's domain grants your users access to Figma, the user interface design platform, and FigJam, the digital whiteboard.

As a Figma administrator, you can manage user permissions, workspaces, teams, and implement controls for your organization's access and privacy.

The basic steps for setting up Figma for Education with SAML SSO in Microsoft Entra ID include:

- [Set up your Figma account](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/set-up-your-figma-account)
- [Set up SAML SSO for Figma with Microsoft Entra ID](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/set-up-saml-sso-figma-microsoft-entra-id)
- [Configure your Figma organization](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/configure-figma-organization)
- [Use Figma in class](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/use-figma-in-class)
