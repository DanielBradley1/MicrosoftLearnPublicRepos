# Find deployment help

There are a few ways to get help with your education deployment.

1. [**FastTrack Center**](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fasttrack/introduction)

   FastTrack provides deployment guidance and assistance for eligible Microsoft 365 Education customers. You must purchase at least 150 eligible licenses per tenant to qualify.

   Eligible education plans include:

   - Microsoft 365 A3
   - Microsoft 365 A5
   - Microsoft Intune for Education


   Additional Microsoft 365 Education plans might also qualify. Review [FastTrack eligibility](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fasttrack/eligibility) for the complete list of eligible plans and requirements. [Get started with FastTrack.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fasttrack/introduction)

2. [**Microsoft Premier support**](https://support.microsoft.com/premier)

   Find out how to access Microsoft's Premier paid support for help with more complex or larger deployments.
3. [**Microsoft Education Support**](https://aka.ms/EduSupport)

   Get help from Microsoft Education Support with deploying and configuring:

   - School Data Sync
   - Microsoft 365 LTI
