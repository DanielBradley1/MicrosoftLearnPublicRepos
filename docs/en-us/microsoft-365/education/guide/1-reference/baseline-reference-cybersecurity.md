# Cybersecurity for education organizations

Protecting student data and maintaining a secure learning environment are critical priorities for educational institutions. This article presents cybersecurity assessment options available to support your organization in evaluating and strengthening your security posture while maximizing the value of Microsoft 365 for Education.

## Education assessment options available

Assessment options available to support your organization include four offerings from Microsoft. The first two focus on improving security while also providing visibility into opportunities to maximize the value of your Microsoft 365 Academic licenses. The remaining two focus specifically on strengthening your security posture. All capabilities included in the last two options are also included in the first two.

Note

Education Assessment isn't available until May 1, 2026.

## Education Security and Value Optimization assessments

The Education Security & Value Optimization Assessment provides educational institutions with an analysis of their security posture and utilization of the capabilities available through their Microsoft 365 Academic licenses.

The assessment highlights the highest-risk gaps across identity, device management, data, and collaboration and identifies opportunities with education-specific capabilities, including Microsoft Learn, Learning Accelerators, Accessibility capabilities, solutions like School Data Sync, Copilot Chat and more. The assessment results provide a view of underutilized or not yet fully deployed capabilities, as well as potential redundant solutions for your consideration, helping your institution gain greater value from its Microsoft 365 Academic investment.

The following table compares the two Education Security & Value Optimization Assessment offerings: the rapid **Self Service Assessment \(SSA\)** and the **Cyber Security Assessment Tool \(CSAT\)**. The SSA is delivered exclusively by QS Solutions in partnership with Microsoft.

### Step 1 - Self Service Assessment \(SSA\)

The rapid self-service assessment gives you an evidence‑based snapshot of your Microsoft 365 Academic environment, and then translates results into step-by-step actions tailored for resource-constrained IT teams. In minutes, assess your tenant against education-relevant controls and receive a prioritized remediation plan mapped to Microsoft Entra, Defender, Intune, and Purview. The self-service assessment also provides a review of the key education capabilities available in your Microsoft 365 Academic licenses.

Findings remain read-only and in-tenant, supporting compliance \(GDPR/FERPA\), measurable progress, and secure AI adoption. Re-run anytime to track Secure Score improvements and brief leadership with a one-page summary.

Key questions answered:

- Is your main administrative account protected with MFA and a separate identity?
- Which unmanaged or non-compliant devices can access student or staff data?
- Where is oversharing exposing sites, Teams, or external guests—and what should be tightened first?
- What opportunities do we have to implement education-specific features \(for example, accessibility, Microsoft Teach, Copilot, Teams, and School Data Sync\)?

Delivered with Microsoft partner QS Solutions, the Education Security and Value Optimization Self-Assessment enables schools to reduce potential security vulnerabilities and identify redundancy and opportunities to get more value from the education capabilities available in your Microsoft 365 Academic licenses.

### Step 2 - Full Education Security and Value Optimization Assessment Tool \(CSAT\)

CSAT for Education is a consultant-led security and governance assessment designed for K-12 and higher education. It analyzes tenant telemetry and configuration to provide an evidence-based risk overview and a prioritized 30/60/90-day remediation plan mapped to Microsoft Entra, Defender, Intune, Purview, and Microsoft 365 Academic. It also highlights opportunities included in Microsoft 365 Academic licenses: collaboration, accessibility, learning tools \(Learning Accelerators, Teach\) and Copilot Chat.

Built for lean IT teams, CSAT supports GDPR/FERPA and regional frameworks \(such as IBP, NIS2\) helps establish guardrails for secure Copilot/AI adoption. Findings remain read-only and in-tenant, enabling faster risk reduction, clear ownership, and measurable progress.

CSAT goes beyond self-service scans by delivering a comprehensive, consultant-led assessment. It produces a defensible risk picture and a tailored remediation plan with clear owners and actionable links.

#### Who should use CSAT

Educational institutions with an Enrollment for Education Solutions \(EES\) agreement with more than 1,000 A3 or A5 Microsoft 365 non-student licenses may benefit from a consultant led engagement. The assessment offers schools and universities seeking measurable risk reduction, especially those with limited IT resources or preparing for secure Copilot/AI and regulatory alignment.

#### Deliverables

- Executive brief: top risks, impact, and prioritized actions
- Deep-dive findings: Identity, Endpoints, Data, Collaboration, Tenant Hygiene \(including Secure Score changes\)
- 30/60/90-day backlog: effort/impact, owners, and direct links for implementation
- Governance baselines: access reviews, external sharing, lifecycle, privileged access
- AI-readiness checks: labeling/DLP, Copilot scopes, data exposure hotspots
- Progress tracking: Secure Score trajectory and risk reduction guidance \(not certification\)
- Microsoft 365 accessibility findings, risks, and recommendations
- Microsoft 365 AI Enablement opportunities
- School Data Sync configuration and implementation options
