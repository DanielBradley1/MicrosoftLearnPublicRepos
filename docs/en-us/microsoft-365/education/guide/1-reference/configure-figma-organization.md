# Configuring your Figma organization

Now that you can log into [figma.com](http://figma.com/) with your admin email, you can see your organization alongside the respective administrator features. Select the **Admin settings** tab. From here, you can see tabs for **Dashboard**, **Workspaces**, plus a list of all **Teams** created, all **Members of the Organization**, **Activity**, **Resources**, and **Settings**.

## Recommended Account Settings

We recommend that you review some of the default settings configured on your district's organization account. The following items are common changes:

- **Team management:** Choose who can create teams in your organization. While many districts allow all users to create their own teams, some districts choose to limit that permission to administrators only.
- **External Access**

  - **Guest Management:** By default, external users can join your organization by invitation. We recommend disabling guest access by selecting the option that **No external users can join your organization**.
  - **Access to external content:** By default, users have access to content outside your organization. We recommend **disabling access to content outside your organization**.
  - **Public Sharing:** By default, public link sharing is disabled. You can choose to enable public sharing by toggling on the option to **Allow links to be shared with anyone**.

    - By default, [open sessions](https://help.figma.com/hc/articles/4410786053911-Invite-visitors-to-an-open-session) are enabled. You can disable by toggling off the option to allow anyone to edit and interact with a FigJam file for 24 hours.

- **Other**

  - **Cursor Chat:** By default, cursor chat is enabled. If you choose to disable this feature, you can toggle off the option to enable cursor chat for all files in your organization.

## Organization Structures

There are various options for organization structure based on your administrative needs. By default, the organization members and teams across sites are all administered by the org admins. If the org admin wants to delegate administrative controls to learning sites, they can create workspaces. Within the workspace structure, the designated workspace admins have administrative control over the teams and users within their group.

Here's a sample of an organization employing a hybrid structure, where one school site uses workspaces and the rest adhere to the default team ownership structure:

[![Picture showing workspace and team only structure.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/images/structure.png)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/images/structure.png#lightbox)

In the following sections, we discuss the **Workspace**, **Team**, and **Membership** controls that help org admins manage their preferred organization structure.

### Workspaces

Workspaces are an optional feature of Figma's enterprise plan. Workspaces allow administrators to divide users into designated groups and delegate administration of those groups to other users.

- This [Help Center](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/7576392133527-Guide-to-workspaces) article covers everything to know about workspace management.
- From the **Workspaces** tab, admins can create new workspaces and review the teams and individual members that belong to a specific workspace. On the **More options** menu, admins can edit ownership of a workspace.
- By toggling **Display workplace selector to** on the **Workspaces** tab, admins can force users to self-select into a workspace upon signing into Figma. If the toggle remains unchecked, admins must manually assign teams or members to workspaces.

For large districts with several school sites, administrators typically create a Workspace with a site-level administrator at each. Students and staff then self-select into their proper workspace upon accessing for the first time.

[![Picture showing examples of workspaces in Figma.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/images/workspaces.png)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/1-reference/images/workspaces.png#lightbox)

### Teams

Teams are how Figma allows teachers or administrators to build groups or classes of students. Teams allow for more streamlined access to shared files and collaborative activities.

- This [Help Center](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039480614-Guide-to-teams) article covers everything to know about team management.
- From the **Teams** tab, admins can create new teams, review owners, project and member counts, as well as reassign org access or workspace membership. On the **More options** menu, administrators can view specific details or reassign ownership of the team.

We recommend each teacher set up a closed team for each of their classes and invite their student to the team via email.

### Members

The **Members** tab shows all members of your organization, their last activity date, roles, and any workspace assignments they have.

- This [Help Center](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/4420842863255-View-members-and-guests) article covers everything to know about Member access management.
- From the **Members** tab, admins can select individual members to upgrade or change their role. On the **More options** menu, administrators can get more insights into the activity of a particular member.
