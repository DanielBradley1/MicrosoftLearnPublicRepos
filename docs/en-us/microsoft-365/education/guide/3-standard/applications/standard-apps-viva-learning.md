# Step 3: Viva Learning

![](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/education/guide/images/sections/standardsm.png)

Microsoft Viva Learning is an integrated learning platform that helps educators and students discover, share, and track learning content within Microsoft Teams. It brings a personalized learning experience to enhance professional development and educational success. This article helps educators and administrators understand the setup, configuration, and benefits of integrating Viva Learning within Microsoft Teams.

## Requirements

- Microsoft A3 license

## Roles and responsibilities

- IT Admin
- Identity Admin
- OneDrive Admin
- SharePoint Admin
- EXO Admin

## Viva Learning in Teams

Viva Learning in Microsoft Teams for Education is a powerful tool designed to enhance the learning experience for students, educators, and administrators by integrating education and professional development seamlessly into the Teams environment. It allows institutions to provide a centralized hub for accessing and managing learning content from a variety of sources, such as Microsoft Learn, LinkedIn Learning, and third-party providers. Viva Learning supports personalized learning paths, making it easier for students to acquire new skills and for educators to engage in continuous professional development. By fostering collaboration and accessibility, Viva Learning enables a dynamic learning ecosystem where educators can assign content, track progress, and create a culture of lifelong learning—all within the familiar Teams interface. This integration not only streamlines learning management but also helps institutions prepare students and staff for future challenges through intuitive and easily accessible educational resources.

Viva Learning integrates with Microsoft Teams to offer a seamless learning experience:

- **Discoverable content**: Access learning resources from Microsoft Learn, LinkedIn Learning, third-party providers, or your organization's custom content.
- **Personalized learning**: Recommendations based on roles, interests, and learning history.
- **Collaborative features**: Share and discuss learning materials directly within Teams chats and channels.

**Key features of Viva Learning:**

- **Content library**: A centralized hub that includes:

  - Microsoft Learn
  - LinkedIn Learning
  - Third-party providers \(for example, Coursera, edX\)
  - Custom content uploaded by your institution

- **Search and discovery**: Use AI-powered tools to find relevant courses.
- **Assignments integration**: Assign learning modules to students and track their progress.
- **Collaboration tools**: Discuss learning topics in Teams channels and share insights with others.

**Benefits of Viva Learning in education:**

- For educators:

  - **Professional development**: Access training to enhance teaching strategies, classroom management, and technical skills.
  - **Content sharing**: Easily share resources with peers or students.
  - **Progress tracking**: Monitor your learning achievements and certifications.

- For students:

  - **Skill building**: Develop competencies aligned with career goals.
  - **Integrated experience**: Learn within Teams without switching platforms.
  - **Engagement**: Access interactive learning content, including videos, modules, and assignments.

- For the educational organization:

  - **Centralized learning**: Streamline access to all learning content in one platform.
  - **Improved engagement**: Foster a culture of continuous learning for educators and students.
  - **Analytics**: Use built-in analytics to measure the impact of learning initiatives.

**How to Use Viva Learning in Teams:**

1. Enable Viva Learning to ensure your institution has access to Viva Learning and that it's enabled in your Microsoft Teams environment.
2. Access Viva Learning.

   1. Open Microsoft Teams.
   2. Select the **Viva Learning** app from the app menu.
   3. Browse or search for content using filters.

3. Share and collaborate.

   - Share learning resources in Teams chats and channels.
   - Use tags to organize content for different groups.

4. Track Progress.

   - Monitor assigned learning tasks through the **My Learning** tab.
   - Educators can review student progress and engagement with courses.

## Create Learning tabs in a Teams channel

Creating Learning tabs in a Teams channel enhances collaboration and streamlines access to important resources for students and educators. By adding dedicated tabs, teachers can integrate tools like Microsoft Lists, OneNote, or third-party apps directly within the channel, enabling a centralized hub for learning materials. For example, a Learning tab could include class notes, assignments, or even interactive apps like quizzes and polls, providing an engaging and organized experience. This functionality supports real-time collaboration, allowing students and educators to co-edit documents, track progress, and access critical resources without navigating away from Teams. Setting up Learning tabs is intuitive and customizable, making it an excellent feature for fostering an interactive and productive learning environment.

Learning tabs in Teams channels allow educators to:

- Centralize learning materials for easy access.
- Integrate third-party educational apps and tools.
- Promote collaboration and engagement through interactive content.

**Benefits of Learning tabs:**

- **Centralized resources**: All materials are available in one location, reducing the need for external tools.
- **Improved engagement**: Interactive tools and apps keep students motivated.
- **Streamlined communication**: Educators can easily share updates and instructions alongside resources.

**How to create Learning tabs in a Teams channel:**

1. **Open the Team channel.** Navigate to the specific Team and channel where you want to create a learning tab.
2. **Select the `+` icon.** At the top of the channel, select the `+` icon to add a new tab.
3. **Choose a tab type.**

   - Select from the list of available apps and resources. Some popular options include:

     - **Microsoft OneNote**: Embed a Class Notebook for collaborative notes and assignments.
     - **Microsoft Forms**: Add forms for quizzes, surveys, or feedback.
     - **SharePoint**: Link to a library of documents, videos, or other materials.
     - **Third-party Apps**: Choose apps like Flipgrid, Kahoot!, or Quizlet for interactive learning experiences.

4. **Configure the tab.**

   - Provide a name for the tab \(for example, "Class Materials" or "Weekly Assignments"\).
   - Set up the content or app settings as needed.

5. **Save and pin the tab.**

   - Select **Save** or **Add Tab** to finalize the setup. The tab now appears at the top of the channel.

**Use cases for Learning tabs in education:**

- **Organized content sharing:** Use Files or a SharePoint tab to share handouts, e-books, or multimedia resources.
- **Interactive Learning Activities:** Add Kahoot!, Quizlet, or Nearpod tabs for gamified learning and real-time engagement.
- **Collaborative Projects:** Create a OneNote Class Notebook tab to allow students to work together on shared projects.
- **Assessments and Feedback:** Use Microsoft Forms tabs for quick quizzes, polls, or surveys.
- **Tracking Progress:** Add an Insights Tab to monitor student activity and engagement within the Team.

**Tips for effective use of Learning tabs:**

- Regularly update tab content to keep it relevant.
- Use clear and consistent naming for tabs to avoid confusion.
- Combine tabs with channel posts to highlight new resources or assignments.

## Microsoft Learn and Microsoft 365 Training Libraries

Microsoft Learn and Microsoft 365 Training Libraries are invaluable resources for educators and students seeking to enhance their digital skills and productivity. Microsoft Learn offers a comprehensive, self-paced learning platform with modules, tutorials, and certifications tailored to a variety of roles and skill levels. It provides interactive lessons on topics such as Power Platform, Dataverse, and Teams, enabling educators to integrate technology effectively into their teaching strategies. Meanwhile, the Microsoft 365 Training Libraries focus on maximizing the potential of Microsoft Office tools like Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Teams, which are essential for collaboration and organization in education. These libraries include detailed guides, video tutorials, and hands-on practice to ensure both teachers and students can efficiently utilize Microsoft 365 applications to enhance learning outcomes and streamline administrative tasks. Together, these platforms empower educational communities to stay competitive in a digital-first world.

### Microsoft Learn for Education

**Microsoft Learn** provides free, interactive, and self-paced training resources to help educators and students master various Microsoft tools and technologies.

**Key Features:**

- **Self-Paced Learning**: Explore at your own speed with interactive modules and learning paths.
- **Certification Preparation**: Study for Microsoft certifications in areas like Azure, Power Platform, and more.
- **Diverse Topics**:

  - Cloud computing
  - Data science and AI
  - App development

- **Gamification**: Earn badges and points for completing courses.
