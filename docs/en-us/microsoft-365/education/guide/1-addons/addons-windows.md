## Universal Print Volume addon \(500 print jobs\)

The Universal Print Volume addon \(500 print jobs\) in education is a service designed to support educational institutions with an increased number of printing needs. As schools, universities, and other educational organizations move towards cloud-based printing solutions, this addon provides an additional 500 print jobs to ensure that teachers, students, and staff can continue to print documents seamlessly. It integrates with Universal Print, a Microsoft 365 service that allows users to print from any device to any printer within the organization’s network, without the need for on-premises print servers. This addon ensures that educational institutions can scale their printing capabilities to meet the demands of a large, distributed student and staff body, improving both productivity and accessibility for everyone in the institution.

**Key features:**

- **500 additional print jobs**: Adds an additional 500 print jobs per month.
- **Cloud-based printing**: Eliminates the need for on-premises print servers.
- **Secure print management**: Supports secure printing through Microsoft 365 identity management.
- **Scalable**: Easily scalable to meet growing print needs in educational institutions.
- **Integrated with Microsoft 365**: Fully integrated with other Microsoft 365 services, allowing easy management and setup.

**Benefits for education:**

- **Cost savings**: Reduces the cost of maintaining physical print servers and other infrastructure.
- **Improved security**: Print jobs are routed securely through the cloud, preventing unauthorized access.
- **Simplified management**: Centralized management through the Universal Print portal makes it easier to monitor and control print usage.

**Ideal use case:**

Educational institutions, including schools and universities, that require additional print capacity due to high student or staff usage, or for large campuses with multiple printing locations.

## High Efficiency Video Codec \(HEVC\)

High Efficiency Video Codec \(HEVC\), also known as H.265, is a video compression standard that provides better video quality at lower bitrates compared to its predecessor, H.264. In the context of education, HEVC is beneficial for delivering high-quality video content with reduced storage requirements and lower bandwidth consumption. This is especially valuable for online learning platforms, virtual classrooms, and video-based educational content, where high-definition video is essential for effective learning experiences. HEVC enables institutions to stream lectures, tutorials, and interactive lessons in 4K or even higher resolution without overwhelming network resources. Additionally, it enhances the overall learning experience by providing clear, crisp visuals for students, even in low-bandwidth environments, and reduces the storage costs for educational institutions that store large amounts of video content for on-demand viewing.

**Benefits of HEVC in education:**

- **Improved streaming quality**: HEVC allows for smoother streaming of video content, even in low bandwidth conditions, which is crucial for remote learning and online classes.
- **Reduced data usage**: HEVC compresses video files more efficiently, which helps in reducing the data consumption for students and institutions, especially in areas with limited internet access.
- **Enhanced video resolution**: HEVC supports higher video resolutions, such as 4K, providing a richer and more immersive learning experience for students using high-definition content.
- **Cost efficiency**: Due to the reduced file sizes and bandwidth requirements, institutions can save on network and storage costs, making it more affordable to distribute high-quality educational videos.
- **Compatibility with modern devices**: Many modern devices, such as smartphones, tablets, and computers, support HEVC, making it easier for students to access educational materials on a wide range of devices.

**Use cases of HEVC in Education:**

- **Virtual classrooms**: HEVC is ideal for online classrooms, allowing teachers to deliver high-quality video lectures without significant lag or buffering, enhancing the student experience.
- **Video-based learning resources**: Educational institutions can use HEVC to store and distribute large video libraries efficiently.
- **Interactive learning**: HEVC enables the use of high-quality videos in simulations, 3D modeling, and virtual reality \(VR\) applications, enhancing interactive learning.
