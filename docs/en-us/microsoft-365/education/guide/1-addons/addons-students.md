## Microsoft 365 A5 Compliance - Students

Microsoft 365 A5 Compliance – Students is an add-on subscription designed to extend advanced compliance capabilities to student users in educational institutions. Based on the details from the modern-workplan-comparison-education.pdf, here’s what it includes and how it applies in education:

**What is Microsoft 365 A5 Compliance – Students?**

This license provides students with access to the same advanced compliance tools available in the full Microsoft 365 A5 Compliance suite, but tailored for student use. It's intended to help institutions meet regulatory, privacy, and data governance requirements while ensuring student data is protected and auditable.

**Included capabilities:**

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| eDiscovery and Audit | Enables advanced eDiscovery \(Standard and Premium\), litigation hold, and audit logs to support legal and compliance investigations. |
| Information Protection & Governance | Includes sensitivity labeling, records management, and auto-classification of content using machine learning. |
| Data Loss Prevention \(DLP\) | Protects sensitive student data in emails, files, and Teams chats by preventing unauthorized sharing. |
| Insider Risk Management | Helps detect and mitigate risky behavior by students \(for example, data exfiltration, policy violations\). |
| Communication Compliance | Monitors student communications for inappropriate or policy-violating content. |
| Customer Lockbox | Ensures that Microsoft support engineers can't access student data without explicit approval. |
| Information Barriers | Prevents communication between specific groups \(for example, students in different programs or age groups\). |

**Licensing notes:**

- This license is marked as “+” in the comparison table, indicating it's available as an add-on to existing student licenses.
- It isn't included by default in A1, A3, or A5 student licenses.
- It complements faculty A5 Compliance licenses, enabling consistent compliance coverage across the institution.

## Microsoft 365 A5 Info Protection and Governance - Students

**What is Microsoft 365 A5 Information Protection and Governance – Students?**

Microsoft 365 A5 Information Protection and Governance – Students is an add-on license that extends advanced data protection and governance capabilities to student accounts. It's designed to help educational institutions meet compliance requirements \(for example, FERPA, GDPR, HIPAA\) and protect sensitive student data across Microsoft 365 services.

This license mirrors the capabilities available to faculty/staff under the A5 Information Protection and Governance suite but is scoped and priced for student use.

**Included capabilities:**

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Azure Information Protection Plan 1 & 2 | Enables classification, labeling, and encryption of documents and emails. |
| Sensitivity Labels | Manual, default, and automatic labeling in Microsoft 365 apps and services \(Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive\). |
| Advanced Classifiers | Use of trainable classifiers, named entities, and contextual detection to apply labels automatically. |
| Container Labeling | Apply sensitivity labels to Teams, SharePoint sites, and Microsoft 365 Groups. |
| Message Encryption | Includes both Basic and Advanced Message Encryption for secure communications. |
| Customer Key | Allows institutions to manage their own encryption keys for added control. |
| Records Management | Enables retention, deletion, and disposition of student data based on policies. |
| Machine Learning-Based Retention | Automatically apply retention labels using AI models. |
| Data Lifecycle Management | Includes manual and rules-based retention policies, Teams retention, and auto-application of labels. |

**Why it matters in education:**

This license helps institutions:

- Protect student personal data, health records, and academic data.
- Automate compliance with data retention and deletion policies.
- Prevent data leaks through DLP and encryption.
- Enable secure collaboration in Teams, OneDrive, and SharePoint.
- Support legal and regulatory audits with eDiscovery and records management.

**Licensing notes:**

- This is an add-on license and isn't included in A1, A3, or A5 student licenses by default.
- It's available only to institutions that have faculty licensed with Microsoft 365 A5 or equivalent.
- It complements other student add-ons like A5 Compliance – Students and A5 Insider Risk Management – Students

## Microsoft 365 A5 Insider Risk Management - Students

**What is Microsoft 365 A5 Insider Risk Management – Students?**

Microsoft 365 A5 Insider Risk Management – Students is an add-on license that brings advanced risk detection and compliance monitoring capabilities to student accounts. It's designed to help educational institutions proactively identify and mitigate risky behaviors, protect sensitive data, and maintain a safe digital learning environment. This license mirrors the faculty version of Microsoft 365 A5 Insider Risk Management but is scoped for student use and priced accordingly.

**Included capabilities:**

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Insider Risk Management | Detects risky student behavior such as data exfiltration, policy violations, or unusual activity patterns. |
| Communication Compliance | Monitors student communications \(for example, Teams, Exchange\) for inappropriate, abusive, or policy-violating content. |
| Information Barriers | Prevents communication between specific student groups \(for example, minors vs. adults, different academic programs\). |
| Customer Lockbox | Ensures Microsoft support can't access student data without explicit institutional approval. |
| Privileged Access Management | Controls and audits elevated access to sensitive student data and systems. |

**Why it matters in education:**

This license helps institutions:

- Protect students and staff from harmful or inappropriate digital interactions.
- Comply with regulations like FERPA, COPPA, and GDPR.
- Detect early warning signs of data leaks, bullying, or insider threats.
- Maintain a safe and inclusive learning environment across Microsoft 365 services.

**Licensing notes:**

- This is an add-on license and isn't included in A1, A3, or A5 student licenses by default 1.
- It's available only to institutions that have faculty licensed with Microsoft 365 A5 or equivalent.
- It complements other student compliance add-ons like A5 Compliance – Students and A5 Information Protection and Governance – Students.

## Microsoft 365 A5 eDiscovery and Audit - Students

Microsoft 365 A5 eDiscovery and Audit – Students is an add-on license that provides advanced legal and compliance investigation tools for student accounts. It enables educational institutions to meet regulatory obligations, respond to legal inquiries, and maintain transparency and accountability in digital learning environments. This license mirrors the faculty version of Microsoft 365 A5 eDiscovery and Audit but is scoped and priced for student use.

**Included capabilities:**

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Content Search | Search across Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams for student data. |
| eDiscovery \(Standard\) | Place holds on student content and export data for legal review. |
| eDiscovery \(Premium\) | Advanced case management, review sets, and machine learning-based relevance scoring. |
| Litigation Hold | Preserve student emails and files for legal or disciplinary investigations. |
| Audit \(Standard\) | Track user and admin activity across Microsoft 365 services. |
| Audit \(Premium\) | Access long-term audit logs and advanced forensic capabilities. |

**Why it matters in education:**

This license helps institutions:

- Respond to legal and disciplinary investigations involving students.
- Comply with regulations like FERPA, GDPR, and HIPAA.
- Preserve and review digital evidence in cases of academic misconduct, harassment, or data breaches.
- Maintain transparency in how student data is accessed and used.

**Licensing notes:**

- This is an add-on license and isn't included in A1, A3, or A5 student licenses by default 1.
- It's available only to institutions that have faculty licensed with Microsoft 365 A5 or equivalent.
- It complements other student compliance add-ons like A5 Compliance – Students and A5 Insider Risk Management – Students

## Microsoft Defender for Endpoint Plan 2 - Students

Microsoft Defender for Endpoint Plan 2 – Students is an add-on security license designed to extend advanced endpoint protection capabilities to student devices in educational institutions. It mirrors the capabilities of the faculty version of Defender for Endpoint Plan 2 but is scoped and priced for student use.

**What it includes:**

| Capability | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Endpoint Detection and Response \(EDR\) | Detects advanced threats and provides behavioral-based analytics to identify suspicious activity on student devices. |
| Threat and Vulnerability Management | Helps identify, assess, and remediate endpoint vulnerabilities in real time. |
| Automated Investigation and Remediation | Uses AI to investigate alerts and take remediation actions automatically. |
| Attack Surface Reduction | Includes exploit protection, controlled folder access, and network protection to reduce attack vectors. |
| Advanced Hunting | Enables custom queries across raw data for proactive threat hunting. |
| Integration with Microsoft 365 Defender | Provides unified visibility and response across endpoints, identities, email, and apps. |

**Why it matters in education:**

This license helps institutions:

- Protect student devices from malware, ransomware, and zero-day attacks.
- Comply with cybersecurity insurance and regulatory requirements \(for example, FERPA, COPPA\).
- Enable secure remote learning by monitoring and securing endpoints outside the school network.
- Support IT teams with automated tools to reduce response time and workload.

**Licensing notes:**

- This is an add-on license and isn't included in A1, A3, or A5 student licenses by default 1.
- It's available only to institutions that have faculty licensed with Microsoft 365 A5 or equivalent.
- It complements other student security add-ons like Microsoft Defender for Identity – Students and Microsoft Defender for Office 365 – Students.
