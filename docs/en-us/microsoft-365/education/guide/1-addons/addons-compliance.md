## Priva Privacy Risk Management

Priva Privacy Risk Management in Education helps educational institutions mitigate privacy risks associated with handling sensitive student and faculty data. This solution offers tools to assess, monitor, and manage privacy risks while ensuring compliance with data protection regulations like Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act \(FERPA\) and General Data Protection Regulation \(GDPR\). In an educational setting, managing privacy risks is critical due to the large amounts of personal data stored across various systems, including student records, grades, and behavioral information. Priva enables educational institutions to identify potential vulnerabilities in their data systems, automate the classification and protection of sensitive information, and proactively manage privacy incidents. By using Priva, institutions can ensure that privacy is maintained across their operations, enhancing trust with students, staff, and parents while minimizing the risk of data breaches or non-compliance penalties. The solution's integration with other Microsoft 365 tools further streamlines data governance and risk management efforts, providing a comprehensive approach to protecting privacy in the educational sector.

It helps institutions comply with privacy regulations such as GDPR and FERPA, and ensures that sensitive student and staff data is protected through risk assessments, controls, and policies. The platform provides functionalities for:

- Tracking privacy risks
- Implementing data protection measures
- Reporting on compliance efforts

## Priva Subject Rights Requests

Priva Subject Rights Requests in education refer to tools and processes that allow educational institutions to efficiently manage requests from individuals \(such as students, parents, or staff\) to exercise their rights over their personal data under privacy regulations like GDPR. These requests often include actions such as access to personal data, data corrections, deletions, or restrictions on how personal data is processed. Priva provides a streamlined way to automate and manage these requests, ensuring that educational organizations comply with privacy laws and regulations while safeguarding student and employee privacy. With Priva, schools and universities can track, manage, and respond to requests in a timely manner, maintaining transparency and fostering trust. This solution simplifies the compliance process, allowing educational institutions to protect sensitive data while respecting the privacy rights of individuals in a digital learning environment.

Priva Subject Rights Requests in the context of education refer to a feature that helps educational institutions manage and respond to data subject requests related to personal data in compliance with privacy regulations, such as GDPR.

These requests typically involve individuals \(such as students, staff, or parents\) exercising their rights over the personal data held by the institution, such as:

- **Right to access**: Requesting access to the personal data an institution holds.
- **Right to rectification**: Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Right to erasure**: Requesting the deletion of personal data when it's no longer necessary.
- **Right to restriction of processing**: Requesting the limitation of how data is processed.
- **Right to data portability**: Requesting the transfer of personal data to another entity in a structured, commonly used format.

## Microsoft Cloud PKI

Microsoft Cloud PKI \(Public Key Infrastructure\) in Education is a modern, cloud-based solution that enables educational institutions to securely manage digital identities, authentication, and data encryption. By using Microsoft’s Azure services, institutions can implement robust PKI systems to provide secure access to resources, applications, and sensitive information for students, faculty, and staff. This solution allows schools to issue and manage digital certificates that authenticate users and devices, ensuring secure communication and data protection across cloud-based platforms. In education, Microsoft Cloud PKI supports compliance with regulations such as FERPA and GDPR, enabling schools to protect student records and administrative data. It simplifies certificate lifecycle management, reduces the burden on IT teams, and ensures that all interactions within the network are encrypted and safe from unauthorized access. With the ability to integrate seamlessly with other Microsoft 365 services, Cloud PKI offers educational institutions a comprehensive security solution that can be easily scaled as their needs evolve.

**In the context of education, Microsoft Cloud PKI enhances security and privacy by enabling:**

- **Secure authentication**: Cloud PKI can be used to manage the identities of students, staff, and faculty securely, enabling authentication for access to various cloud-based resources, applications, and services.
- **Email encryption**: By using digital certificates, it can encrypt emails to ensure confidentiality when communicating sensitive information like grades, assignments, and personal data.
- **Secure document signing**: Faculty and administrators can digitally sign documents to ensure authenticity and integrity, making it easy to verify documents' legitimacy in a digital-first education environment.
- **Integration with Microsoft 365**: Microsoft Cloud PKI integrates with Microsoft 365 apps, enhancing security by allowing users to digitally sign documents, authenticate identity, and encrypt emails seamlessly within the Microsoft ecosystem.
- **Cost-effective scalability**: Being a cloud-based solution, it offers the scalability and flexibility that educational institutions need, reducing the need for on-premises infrastructure and management costs.
- **Enhanced security compliance**: For schools and universities that need to comply with data protection regulations \(like FERPA\), Cloud PKI ensures that communications and data handling practices are secure and compliant with privacy standards.

**Key benefits for education:**

- **Secure access to online resources**: Ensures that only authorized users can access educational platforms and cloud-based services.
- **Streamlined digital signatures**: Simplifies workflows for document signing for administrative tasks, contracts, and approvals.
- **Improved privacy for sensitive student data**: Ensures that confidential student information remains protected.
- **Ease of scalability**: Easily scales with the institution’s needs, accommodating growing user bases and new use cases.
