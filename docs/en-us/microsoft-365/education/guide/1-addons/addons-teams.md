# Microsoft Teams addons for education

These addons provide advanced features and capabilities that support virtual learning, improve communication, and streamline administrative tasks. This article explores the various Microsoft Teams addons available for educational institutions, detailing their key features, benefits, and ideal use cases to help you make the most of your Teams environment.

## Teams Premium

Microsoft Teams Premium enhances the core capabilities of Microsoft Teams by offering advanced features tailored to meet the unique needs of educational institutions. It's designed to create secure, engaging, and personalized learning environments while simplifying management for educators and administrators. With Teams Premium, schools can take advantage of advanced meeting customization, such as branding and unique meeting templates, to create a professional and consistent experience for classes, virtual events, and parent-teacher conferences. Enhanced security features, including end-to-end encryption for meetings and sensitivity labels, ensure that sensitive student information and discussions remain protected. Teams Premium also provides AI-powered tools like intelligent recap, live translations, and automated note-taking, helping educators save time and focus on teaching. By enabling deeper insights and integrations, Teams Premium supports a more interactive and effective learning experience, making it an invaluable tool for modern education.

**Key features of Teams Premium in education:**

- **Enhanced virtual classrooms**

  - Advanced meeting controls, such as custom meeting templates for lectures, office hours, or parent-teacher conferences.
  - **Breakout rooms**: Automatically assign students into predefined groups for collaborative learning.
  - **Attendance tracking**: Generate detailed attendance reports to monitor student participation.

- **AI-powered tools**

  - **Intelligent recap**: AI-generated meeting summaries with highlights, action items, and speaker attributions.
  - **Live translations**: Real-time translation of meeting captions to improve accessibility and inclusivity for diverse classrooms.

- **Customization and branding**

  - Custom meeting backgrounds and logos to reflect school or institution branding.
  - Personalized learning environments for students and educators.

- **Advanced security and compliance**

  - End-to-end encryption for sensitive conversations and educational data.
  - Enhanced security features, including watermarks and meeting access control, to prevent unauthorized sharing of content.

- **Scheduling and communication**

  - Virtual Appointments to facilitate streamlined scheduling for office hours, student counseling, or administrative meetings.
  - Integrated chat tools to enhance teacher-student communication.

- **Analytics and reporting**

  - Access to detailed meeting and user engagement analytics.
  - Insights on student participation and performance during virtual sessions.

- **Integration with LMS**

  - Seamless integration with popular Learning Management Systems \(LMS\) for a unified education platform.

**Ideal use cases:**

- **Educational institutions**: Schools, colleges, and universities seeking to enhance virtual and hybrid learning experiences.
- **Teachers and professors**: To deliver engaging and secure online lectures and tutorials.
- **Administrators**: To manage secure communication and collaboration across the institution.
- **Students**: To participate in dynamic and interactive virtual learning environments.

**Learn more about Teams Premium:**

- [**Microsoft Teams Premium**](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-teams/premium) is an upgraded version of Microsoft Teams that provides additional features to enhance security, meeting experiences, and collaboration.

## Audio Conferencing

Microsoft Teams Audio Conferencing in education provides a seamless and accessible way for students, educators, and administrators to connect and collaborate from virtually anywhere. This feature enables participants to join meetings via a phone call when internet access or device compatibility is limited, ensuring inclusivity and flexibility. With a dedicated dial-in number and dial-out capabilities, educators can host interactive lectures, virtual office hours, or parent-teacher conferences without relying solely on video conferencing. Audio conferencing supports engagement in remote and hybrid learning environments, fosters effective communication, and bridges the gap for participants who may face technological challenges, helping schools and universities maintain consistent and productive collaboration.

**Key features:**

- **Dial-in access**: Students, faculty, and staff can join meetings using a dedicated phone number, ensuring participation from remote or low-connectivity areas.
- **Dial-out capability**: Meeting organizers can call participants directly from the meeting, helping to include those unable to connect via traditional methods.
- **Global coverage**: Provides local and toll-free numbers across many regions, supporting international education programs and collaborations.
- **Seamless integration**: Works natively within the Microsoft Teams platform, streamlining setup and use for educators and administrators.
- **Enhanced accessibility**: Ensures inclusivity by providing an audio-only option for participants with limited technological resources.

**Benefits for education:**

- **Increased accessibility**: Ensures all participants, regardless of their device or internet availability, can join and engage in educational sessions.
- **Support for remote learning**: Facilitates connection in hybrid or fully remote learning environments.
- **Flexibility for educators**: Teachers can conduct classes or meetings even in situations where internet connectivity is unstable.
- **Collaboration beyond the classroom**: Enables collaboration with guest speakers, international partners, or students in different regions.
- **Improved reliability**: Provides a backup option to ensure meeting continuity during technical disruptions.

**Use case example:**

A university professor can schedule a Teams meeting for a virtual lecture. Students without internet access can join the session by dialing a local phone number, allowing them to participate in real-time discussions and Q&A.

**Licensing and availability:**

Teams Audio Conferencing is included in certain Microsoft 365 Education licenses or can be purchased as an addon. Check with your institution's IT department or Microsoft representative for details.

## Teams Phone Standard

Microsoft Teams Phone Standard in Education is a communication solution designed to enhance collaboration and connectivity within schools, colleges, and universities. It integrates seamlessly into Microsoft Teams, enabling educators, students, and administrators to make and receive calls through a unified platform. Teams Phone Standard provides essential telephony features such as voicemail, call transfer, call queues, and auto attendants, allowing institutions to replace traditional phone systems with a modern, cloud-based solution. This service supports both internal and external calling, ensuring streamlined communication across campuses and with external stakeholders. By integrating with existing Teams features like chat, video conferencing, and file sharing, Teams Phone Standard fosters a cohesive and efficient communication ecosystem. Ideal for educational environments, it promotes accessibility, enhances productivity, and reduces the complexity and costs associated with managing traditional phone systems.

**Key features:**

- **Cloud-based calling**: Enables inbound and outbound calling to public-switched telephone networks \(PSTN\) directly from Microsoft Teams.
- **Call management**: Provides features such as call forwarding, voicemail, caller ID, and call transfer.
- **Seamless integration**: Fully integrates with the Microsoft Teams environment, supporting both individual and group calls for educators and administrators.
- **Education-specific benefits**: Supports academic workflows and improves communication between staff, parents, and students.
- **Scalability**: Designed to handle small to large-scale deployments, catering to the varying needs of educational institutions.

**Benefits for education:**

- **Cost efficiency**: Reduces reliance on traditional telephony systems and hardware, minimizing costs.
- **Enhanced collaboration**: Encourages streamlined communication between faculty, students, and parents within the Teams environment.
- **Remote and hybrid learning support**: Facilitates smooth communication in virtual learning scenarios.
- **Improved accessibility**: Ensures that all stakeholders have access to modern telephony features from anywhere, using any device.

**Licensing and requirements:**

- Teams Phone Standard is offered as an addon license to existing Microsoft 365 A1, A3, or A5 Education subscriptions.
- A **Microsoft Calling Plan**, **Direct Routing**, or a **Operator Connect** partner is required to enable PSTN calling functionality.

## Domestic Calling Plan

The Microsoft Teams Domestic Calling Plan for education provides schools, colleges, and universities with a reliable and efficient telephony solution integrated directly within the Teams platform. This plan enables educators, administrators, and staff to make and receive calls to and from traditional phone numbers, both within the institution and across the country/region, using their Teams-enabled devices. With features such as call transfer, voicemail, call queues, and auto-attendants, the domestic calling plan enhances communication and collaboration within educational institutions. By consolidating phone services into the Microsoft Teams ecosystem, schools can streamline operations, reduce costs associated with traditional phone systems, and provide a unified communication experience that supports remote and hybrid learning environments. This solution is especially beneficial for connecting faculty, staff, and stakeholders while ensuring secure and seamless communication.m.

**Key features:**

- **Make and receive calls**: Users can make and receive domestic calls to and from landlines, mobile phones, and other telecommunication devices within the country/region.
- **Integrated with Microsoft Teams**: This plan is fully integrated into the Microsoft Teams environment, so users can place and receive calls using their Teams interface without needing separate systems.
- **Cost-effective for educational institutions**: It offers an affordable calling solution for educational institutions with predictable pricing based on usage, suitable for schools, colleges, and universities.
- **Call forwarding and voicemail**: It includes features such as call forwarding, voicemail, and auto-attendants, ensuring efficient communication in an educational setting.
- **Supports emergency calling**: The Teams Domestic Calling Plan ensures that users can make emergency calls when needed, with location-based routing for emergency services.

**Benefits:**

- **Simplified communication**: Teams Domestic Calling Plan centralizes communication tools, enabling educators, students, and staff to make and receive calls directly through Teams.
- **Reduced costs**: It eliminates the need for traditional phone systems, reducing the overall communication expenses for educational organizations.
- **Security and compliance**: Teams Domestic Calling Plan meets education-specific regulatory requirements, ensuring data protection and privacy compliance.

**Prerequisites:**

- The Teams Domestic Calling Plan is available as part of Microsoft 365 Education or Microsoft 365 A3/A5 plans.
- A compatible Microsoft Teams license is required for all users who will be making or receiving calls.

**Limitations:**

- **Domestic Calls Only**: This plan only supports domestic calling within the country/region. International calls require an additional addon plan.
- **Availability**: Availability of the Domestic Calling Plan may vary by country or region.

**Ideal use cases:**

- Schools, colleges, and universities looking to streamline voice communications.
- Educational organizations needing to replace outdated phone systems with a more integrated and cost-effective solution.

## Pay-as-you-go Calling Plan

The Teams Pay-as-you-go Calling Plan in education provides a flexible and cost-effective solution for schools, colleges, and universities to enable voice calling capabilities through Microsoft Teams. This plan eliminates the need for large upfront investments in traditional telephony systems by allowing institutions to pay only for the calling minutes they use. With integration into Microsoft Teams, educators, administrators, and staff can make and receive calls seamlessly, leveraging a cloud-based solution that simplifies communication and collaboration. This pay-as-you-go model is particularly beneficial for educational institutions with fluctuating communication needs, such as seasonal peaks during enrollment or exam periods. By combining affordability with the reliability of Microsoft's infrastructure, the plan supports efficient communication while helping institutions optimize their budgets.

**Key features:**

- **Unlimited domestic calling:** Users can make and receive unlimited calls within the country or region of operation.
- **Integration with Microsoft Teams:** Fully integrates with Teams, allowing voice calls directly within the platform.
- **Centralized management:** Administrators can manage numbers, users, and calling features through the Microsoft 365 Admin Center.
- **Call quality and reliability:** Built on Microsoft's robust cloud infrastructure for high-quality voice calls.
- **Voicemail services:** Includes voicemail with transcription capabilities.
- **Call forwarding and delegation:** Supports advanced call handling options like forwarding, delegation, and simultaneous ringing.
- **Emergency calling support:** Provides emergency calling services compliant with local regulations.
- **Easy scalability:** Scales to meet the needs of small groups or large organizations with flexible licensing.

**Ideal use cases in education:**

- **Faculty and staff communication:** Enhance connectivity between faculty, staff, and administration through reliable voice calling.
- **Parent and guardian outreach:** Simplify communications with parents and guardians for meetings, updates, or emergencies.
- **Student support services:** Facilitate direct contact with students for academic advising, counseling, or technical support.
- **Campus-wide integration:** Connect all users under a single telephony and collaboration platform, reducing reliance on traditional phone systems.

**Benefits:**

- Reduces the cost and complexity of maintaining on-premises PBX systems.
- Streamlines communication across educational institutions with a unified platform.
- Enhances collaboration by integrating voice calling with chat, video, and document sharing in Teams.
- Complies with local telephony and regulatory requirements.

## International Calling Plan

Microsoft Teams International Calling Plan in Education is a feature designed to enable educational institutions to enhance communication and collaboration globally. By integrating voice calling into Microsoft Teams, schools, colleges, and universities can simplify their telephony systems, replacing traditional phone setups with a cloud-based solution. The plan allows educators, administrators, and support staff to make and receive international calls directly from Microsoft Teams, ensuring seamless communication across borders. This is valuable for institutions with international students, global research partnerships, or cross-border administrative needs. The calling plan is equipped with enterprise-grade security, centralized management, and scalable options, making it an efficient and cost-effective solution for modern educational environments. With Teams International Calling Plan, institutions can unify their communication infrastructure, fostering a connected and inclusive educational experience.

**Key features:**

- **International call access**: Enables Teams users to call phone numbers outside the organization, including international numbers.
- **Cost-effective global calling**: Institutions can provide international calling at competitive rates, with options for toll-free numbers and call plans tailored to the needs of educational environments.
- **Integration with Microsoft Teams**: The calling plan is fully integrated with Teams, allowing users to make international calls directly from Teams without needing external systems or apps.
- **Flexible pricing**: Offers different pricing tiers based on the number of international minutes required, which can be customized for different institutional needs.
- **Manageability**: Allows educational administrators to manage calling plans and monitor usage through the Microsoft 365 Admin Center.
- **Global reach**: Supports a wide range of countries/regions and territories, ensuring that institutions can maintain communication worldwide.

## Teams Phone with Domestic Calling Plan

Teams Phone with Domestic Calling Plan is a robust communication solution designed for educational institutions to enhance collaboration and streamline communication. By integrating seamlessly with Microsoft Teams, this feature allows faculty, staff, and administrators to make and receive phone calls directly from their Teams interface, using their institution-assigned phone number. The Domestic Calling Plan includes a predefined number of minutes for calls within the country/region, providing a reliable and cost-effective telephony service without the need for traditional phone systems.

For schools, colleges, and universities, Teams Phone simplifies communication by offering centralized management, robust security, and high-quality audio, all within a familiar platform. Whether for scheduling parent-teacher meetings, coordinating with staff, or conducting administrative calls, this solution ensures uninterrupted communication. Additionally, educators can leverage Teams Phone to enhance accessibility for students and parents, promoting a connected and collaborative learning environment. By combining modern telephony features with the power of Microsoft Teams, the Teams Phone with Domestic Calling Plan helps educational institutions stay connected while reducing operational complexity.

**Key features:**

- **Seamless calling experience**: Use Microsoft Teams as your primary calling solution, integrating voice and collaboration in one app.
- **Domestic calling**: Includes unlimited calls to and from the United States and Canada, reducing communication costs for educational institutions.
- **Phone numbers**: Institutions can assign phone numbers to users or allow students and staff to bring their own numbers \(porting\).
- **Call management**: Features like call forwarding, voicemail, and call transfer are available for a smoother calling experience.
- **Emergency calling**: Supports emergency calling services, ensuring compliance with regulations regarding emergency access.
- **Integration with Teams**: Fully integrates with Microsoft Teams for easy access to calling, meetings, and collaboration.
- **Cloud-based solution**: No need for on-premises hardware. The solution operates in the cloud, ensuring easy scalability and management.
- **Cost-effective**: A budget-friendly solution for educational institutions, as it eliminates the need for traditional phone systems and long-distance calling charges.
- **Admin control**: IT administrators have full control over call settings, user management, and billing via the Teams Admin Center.
