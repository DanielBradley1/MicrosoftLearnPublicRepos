# Data Residency for Microsoft 365 Copilot \(including Cowork\)

This page is related to the [Location of Customer Data at Rest for Core Online Services](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/PrivacyandSecurityTerms/all) and helps enumerate the specific conditions and exclusions for Microsoft 365 Copilot & Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat and select components \(such as Cowork\).

## Microsoft 365 Copilot

- No modifications

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

- No modifications

## Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork

Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork provides a commitment to store content of interactions \(prompts and responses\) at rest when a [*Tenant*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) has a [*Default Geography*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) in the following countries/regions:

> Australia, Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, India, Japan, Norway, South Africa, South Korea, Sweden, Switzerland, United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, and United States

Note

Workspace files and custom skills are stored in OneDrive / SharePoint Embedded under the SharePoint/OneDrive [*Data Residency*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) commitments.

Note

These provisions apply to all *Tenants* including *Tenants* who have purchased [*Advanced Data Residency \(ADR\)*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) and/or [*Multi-Geo*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions).
