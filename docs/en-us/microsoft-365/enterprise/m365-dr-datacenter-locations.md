# Microsoft 365 datacenter locations

This article provides country or region and city-level detail for Microsoft 365 datacenter locations. Use this information to understand where your [*Customer Data*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) may be stored based on your [*Tenant's*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) [*Geography*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions).

For definitions of italicized terms, see [Key terms and definitions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide).

## Overview

The [*Data Location Card*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) in the Microsoft 365 admin center displays simplified *Geography* names \(such as "France" or "European Union/EFTA"\) due to display space constraints and overall user experience considerations. This page provides the detailed datacenter location information behind those *Geography* names.

Note

For security and compliance reasons, Microsoft doesn't disclose the exact street addresses of its datacenters. The table below provides detail to the metropolitan/city level to support transparency around [*Data Residency*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions).

## How to use this information

When your *Data Location Card* displays a *Geography* name:

- **For [*Local Region Geographies*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions)** \(such as "France" or "Japan"\): Your *Customer Data* is stored in datacenters within that specific country or region, in the cities listed in the Local Region Geographies table above.
- **For [*Macro Region Geographies*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions)** \(such as "Americas" or "European Union/EFTA"\): Your *Customer Data* is stored in datacenters within that region at one or more of the locations listed in the Macro Region Geographies table above. Where applicable, the table identifies both the country or region and city.
