# Compare Microsoft 365 Data Residency offerings

Microsoft offers five [*Data Residency*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) solutions for Microsoft 365 customers. Two are included for eligible [*Tenants*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) at no additional cost, while three are paid add-on services for organizations with expanded or complex requirements. This article compares all five offerings to help you determine which solution best meets your organization's requirements.

For definitions of italicized terms, see [Key terms and definitions](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide).

### Included commitments for eligible tenants

These commitments are included for *Tenants* that meet specific eligibility criteria. No additional purchase is required. Select commercial *Tenants* can configure whether their in-country *Product Terms* commitment is active.

- **Product Terms**: A [*Durable Commitment on Data Location*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) specified in the [Product Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/PrivacyandSecurityTerms/all) for eligible *Tenants* with a [*Default Geography*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) in specific countries or regions. For select commercial *Tenants*, the **Store Microsoft 365 customer data in-country** setting controls whether the in-country commitment is active. For eligibility and configuration details, see [Data residency setting for commercial customers in select geographies](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-product-terms?view=o365-worldwide#data-residency-setting-for-commercial-customers-in-select-geographies).
- **European Union Data Boundary \(EUDB\)**: A commitment to store and process [*Customer Data*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) within the EU/EFTA region for eligible *Tenants* with a sign-up location in a country or region in the EU or EFTA.

### Paid add-on services

These services require separate licensing and provide additional capabilities beyond the included commitments.

- ***Advanced Data Residency \(ADR\)***: A paid add-on that expands service coverage and provides data migration services for [*Local Region Geographies*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions).
- ***Advanced Data Residency for Education \(ADR-E\)***: A paid add-on for eligible Education customers that provides *Data Residency* and migration services for core workloads in *Local Region Geographies*.
- ***Multi-Geo Capabilities***: A paid add-on that enables organizations to store *Customer Data* across multiple [*Geographies*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) within a single *Tenant*.

The commitments don't cover identical data or operations. In particular, [*EUDB*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) includes processing commitments and additional data categories, while the other offerings primarily provide commitments for specified *Customer Data* stored at rest. Review the offering-specific documentation before making a compliance or purchasing decision.

## How Customer Data can move between Geographies

Once a Microsoft 365 service provisions a *Tenant* into a particular *Geography*, your *Customer Data* can move to another *Geography* in three ways. Understanding these methods helps you select the offering that aligns with your data movement requirements.

| Method | Trigger | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ***ADR* migration** | *Tenant* has a *Default Geography* that is also a *Local Region Geography* and subscribes to *Advanced Data Residency* | *Customer Data* for *Microsoft 365 Core Services* and [*Microsoft 365 Expanded Services*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) migrates from its [*Current Geography*](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-key-terms-definitions?view=o365-worldwide#table-12-key-terms-and-definitions) to the *Local Region Geography* corresponding to the *Tenant's* *Default Geography*. |
| ***Multi-Geo* assignment** | *Tenant* subscribes to *Multi-Geo Capabilities* | The Global Administrator can assign each user's *Customer Data* for Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive, Microsoft Teams, and Microsoft 365 Copilot to *Satellite Geographies*. |
| **Microsoft-managed \(no commitment\)** | *Tenant* doesn't have a *Durable Commitment on Data Location* | *Customer Data* isn't committed to reside in any particular datacenter. Microsoft stores data in a location that enables effective service delivery, and that location is subject to change without notice. |

For implementation details, see [Choose the right Microsoft 365 Data Residency solution](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/enterprise/m365-dr-choose-solution?view=o365-worldwide).
