# Add your company branding to the Microsoft 365 sign-in page

For more small business content, see [Small business help & learning](https://go.microsoft.com/fwlink/?linkid=2224585).

You can now use the Microsoft Entra subscription that is included with your Microsoft 365 subscription to customize the sign-in page your users see.

## Add company branding to your sign-in page and Access Panel pages

If you have a paid subscription to Microsoft 365 for business, Microsoft Dynamics CRM Online, Enterprise Mobility Suite, or other Microsoft services, you have a free subscription to Microsoft Entra ID. Use Microsoft Entra ID to create and manage user and group accounts, and add company branding to your pages. To activate this subscription and access the Microsoft Azure Management Portal, complete a one-time registration process. Afterward, you can access Microsoft Entra ID from your Microsoft service that uses it.

For instructions on how to register your Microsoft 365 subscription, see [Register your free Microsoft Entra subscription](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/compliance/use-your-free-azure-ad-subscription-in-office-365?view=o365-worldwide). For general management instructions, see [Manage the directory for your Microsoft 365 subscription in Azure](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/fundamentals/active-directory-how-subscriptions-associated-directory).

The following figure shows which parts of the sign-in page you can modify in Azure.

![Screenshot of the Microsoft 365 sign-in page showing customizable areas including the large illustration, banner logo, and text area.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/media/screenshotbranding.png?view=o365-worldwide)

1. The large illustration and/or its background color.
2. The banner logo.
3. You can also add text to this area.

In addition to the sign-in page, you can customize the Access Panel page in Azure.

## Next steps

If you're ready to add branding, explore the customization options in the Azure content set: [Add company branding to your Sign-in and Access Panel pages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/active-directory/fundamentals/customize-branding).
