# Access Microsoft 365 admin settings in your LMS

As an LMS admin, you must enable the new app in the Microsoft 365 LTI Admin Settings. Follow these LMS-specific instructions to enable the preview for your teachers.

## Canvas

1. As a Canvas Administrator, select the **Admin** icon on the left bar, and then select the top-level account where the tool was deployed.
2. Select the **Settings** link in the left side navigation, and then select the **Apps** tab at the top to show the **External Apps** page. Select the **View App Configurations** button in the header to show the list of your deployed apps.
3. Locate the **Microsoft Education** app and select the **Gear** icon on the right to show the menu. Select the **Configure** option to show the Microsoft Education admin configuration dialog.

   ![Screenshot of the Microsoft Education app gear icon menu in Canvas, with the Configure option selected.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/canvas-gear-menu-configure-option.png?view=o365-worldwide)
4. In the **Applications** list, switch the **Copilot teaching tools** toggle to **Enabled teaching tools**.

   ![Screenshot of the Copilot teaching tools setting with its toggle switched on.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/copilot-teaching-tools-preview-toggle.png?view=o365-worldwide)

## Blackboard

1. Sign in to Blackboard as an administrator and select **Admin** in the bottom left toolbar.

   ![Screenshot of the Admin option selected in the Blackboard bottom left toolbar for administrator access.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/blackboard-admin-menu-selection.png?view=o365-worldwide)
2. In the **Tools and Utilities** menu, select the **Microsoft Education Admin Settings**.

   ::image type="content" source="media/blackboard-admin-tools-utilities-menu.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the Blackboard Tools and Utilities menu with Microsoft Education Admin Settings highlighted.":::
3. In the **Applications** list, switch the **Copilot teaching tools** toggle to **Enabled teaching tools**.

   ![Screenshot of the Copilot teaching tools setting with its toggle switched on.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/copilot-teaching-tools-preview-toggle.png?view=o365-worldwide)

## Schoology

1. Sign in to Schoology as an administrator and select the **App Center** icon in the top toolbar. Select the **Microsoft Education** app to access **Admin Settings**.

   ![Screenshot of the Schoology App Center menu opened from the top toolbar, showing the Microsoft Education app and an App Center button.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/schoology-app-center-microsoft-education.jpg?view=o365-worldwide)
2. Select your profile picture in the top right to access the **Admin Configuration**.
3. In the **Applications** list, switch the **Copilot teaching tools** toggle to **Enabled teaching tools**.

   ![Screenshot of the Copilot teaching tools setting with its toggle switched on.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/copilot-teaching-tools-preview-toggle.png?view=o365-worldwide)

## D2L/Brightspace

1. Sign in to Brightspace as an administrator and select any course in your **My Courses** list.
2. In the course toolbar, select the **Microsoft Education** link to open the Microsoft 365 LTI homepage. You might need to follow the instructions in [this article](https://community.d2l.com/brightspace/kb/articles/1700-customize-your-course-navbar) to add the link to your course navbar.
3. Select your profile picture on the top right to access the **Admin Configuration**.
4. In the **Applications** list, switch the **Copilot teaching tools** toggle to **Enabled teaching tools**.

   ![Screenshot of the Copilot teaching tools setting with its toggle switched on.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/copilot-teaching-tools-preview-toggle.png?view=o365-worldwide)

## Moodle

1. Sign in to Moodle as an administrator and access any course in **Edit Mode** where the Microsoft 365 LTI is added as an activity.
2. Select your profile picture on the top right of the app, and choose **Admin Settings**.
3. In the **Applications** list, switch the **Copilot teaching tools** toggle to **Enabled teaching tools**.

   ![Screenshot of the Copilot teaching tools setting with its toggle switched on.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/lti/media/copilot-teaching-tools-preview-toggle.png?view=o365-worldwide)
