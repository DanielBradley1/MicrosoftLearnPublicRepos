# Ask Microsoft quoting FAQ

Use this FAQ to learn how the agent-led quoting experience works, which products are eligible, and what to expect after you create a quote.

You can use the Ask Microsoft web agent on Microsoft 365 commercial sites to create a quote. This provides an alternative to purchasing directly online or working with a sales representative by chat or phone.

Quoting is currently available in the United States, Australia, and New Zealand for small and medium-sized businesses purchasing eligible Microsoft 365 per-user products. Certain enterprise products, as well as individual and family plans, are not supported.

## Getting started with quoting

### What are the benefits of quoting?

Quoting lets you save product selections, share pricing for review or approval, and return to purchase later. It is especially helpful for multi-product purchases because you can complete one checkout instead of separate checkout flows for each product.

### Is pricing different when I request a quote instead of buying online?

No. Pricing is the same whether you request a quote or buy online, and quoting does not provide additional discounts.

### What is the difference between getting a quote and buying directly?

Buying directly lets you complete your purchase online immediately. A quote lets you save product selections, share pricing for review or approval, and return to purchase later. Quotes are especially useful when you are buying multiple products or need more time to decide. Pricing is the same for both options.

### Which option is right for me, quoting or direct purchase?

Choose direct purchase if you are ready to buy now and do not need internal review. Choose quoting if you need to share pricing, obtain approval, or simplify a later checkout for multiple products.

### How long does it take to create a quote?

Creating a quote takes only a few minutes. If no additional validation is required, you will receive a checkout link right away. Otherwise, you will receive a quote ID and further information by email. In either case, a sales representative will follow up within 48 business hours to help you review the quote and move forward.

### Can I request a quote if I am not currently a Microsoft customer?

Yes! New Microsoft customers can request a quote. After the quote is created, follow the provided steps, which may include creating a new tenant. A sales representative can also help answer questions and guide you through the next steps.

### How do I start a quote?

Request a quote from the Ask Microsoft agent, then describe the business products you need and the number of licenses. Review the products, quantities, subscription terms, and billing options. When the estimate is correct, confirm your selections and provide an email address to receive the quote.

### What if I am only comparing plans and am not ready to buy?

The Ask Microsoft agent can explain and compare plans without creating a quote. Create a quote when you want to save specific selections or share them for internal review. Creating a quote does not require you to purchase.

## Quoting - Pricing, Eligibility and Discounts

### Can I receive nonprofit, education, or government pricing through quoting?

No. Nonprofit, education, and government customers are not supported through this quoting experience. Visit the relevant Microsoft site to check eligibility, view applicable pricing, or contact Sales.

### Are business discounts available through quoting?

No. Quoting does not provide additional discounts; pricing matches the prices available on the Microsoft website.

### Why are web promotions not included in my quote?

A sales representative applies eligible web promotions. After you confirm the quote, a sales representative will contact you to collect any required details and apply any applicable web promotions.

### Can I get a quote to upgrade from Business Basic to Business Premium?

Upgrades aren't supported through quoting. You can upgrade directly in the Microsoft 365 admin center.

### My company works with a Microsoft partner. Can I still use quoting?

If your company works with a Microsoft partner, contact the partner directly for help with your purchase.

### Can I use this quoting experience if my company has an Enterprise Agreement \(EA\) or create a quote for internal review only?

No. This quoting experience is not available to customers with an Enterprise Agreement \(EA\). Contact your Microsoft account manager for the appropriate quoting and purchasing support.

### Which products are eligible for quoting?

Microsoft 365 Business and Enterprise suite products are generally eligible for quoting, with some exclusions. Frontline, government, nonprofit, and education SKUs may require additional eligibility checks, so contact a seller for assistance.

Consumer products are not currently supported through this quoting experience.

### Can I request a quote for Microsoft 365 Individual or Family plans?

No. Individual and Family plans are consumer products and are not supported through this business quoting experience. Visit the Microsoft website to review and purchase available consumer plans.

### Does my selected plan or bundle include Microsoft 365 Copilot or Teams?

Do not assume every Microsoft 365 plan includes Copilot or Teams. Review the exact product or bundle name and its listed inclusions. If the quote contains an add-on, a sales representative will verify any required base licenses before providing a checkout link.

### Can I buy an add-on without the required base subscription?

An add-on may require an eligible base subscription. A sales representative will verify the prerequisite licenses that the organization might already have before providing a checkout link.

### Can I request or resume a trial through quoting?

Trials are not currently supported through the quoting experience. Use the product's separate trial sign-up process or contact a sales representative for help with an existing trial. You can still request a paid quote for an eligible product.

### I want to purchase Azure and Microsoft 365. Can I receive one quote for both?

Azure is not supported through this quoting experience. You can request a quote for Microsoft 365, but you will need to purchase Azure separately through the Microsoft Azure site.

### Can I create multiple quotes with different products and quantities for internal review?

Yes. You can create multiple quotes to compare different products or quantities for internal review. Repeat the quoting steps for each version you want to create.

### Can I include Microsoft 365 and Power BI Pro in the same quote?

Yes, you can add Microsoft 365 and Power BI Pro to the same quote.

### Where can I see applicable taxes and fees?

Taxes and fees are shown during checkout. After you confirm the quote and open the checkout link, sign in or continue as directed to review the products and final total. Applicable taxes are calculated at purchase based on your billing address and local tax rules.

### Does the draft quote include taxes?

Taxes are not finalized in the draft quote. Applicable taxes are calculated at purchase based on your billing address and local tax rules and appear in the final total.

### Why is the price shown in a different currency?

Quoting is currently supported only in Australia, New Zealand, and the United States. Prices and product availability reflect the regional Microsoft site you are using. To see another market's pricing and products, switch to that market's site and start a new quote.

### What should I do if I start a quote and need to change markets?

Select "End quoting experience," switch to the appropriate market's site, and start a new quote using that market's plans and pricing. Quoting is currently supported only in Australia, New Zealand, and the United States.

### If I currently have a Microsoft 365 Business Premium license, can I convert it to the one with Copilot?

The quoting experience can help you create a quote for supported products. If you already have Microsoft 365 Business Premium and are looking to add Copilot or make changes to your existing licenses, a sales representative can help review your current licensing and determine the appropriate option.

## Quote details and management

### Is the quoted price locked, or can it change?

The quote reflects the current price when it is created, but the price is not locked. It may change if the quote expires, products or quantities are updated, or Microsoft pricing or promotions change.

### Is the quote linked to my existing Microsoft account?

The quote is initially created using your name and contact email. Your Microsoft tenant is linked later in the purchase process when you sign in through the checkout link. A sales representative can help confirm that everything is set up correctly and answer any questions.

### Can I retrieve my quote using the quote ID?

No. Quotes cannot be retrieved in chat, even with a quote ID. A copy of the quote and its ID is sent to the email address you provided.

### Can I change the quote after I receive it?

Yes. After you receive the draft quote by email, ask the sales representative who follows up with you to update products, quantities, or other details.

### Can I cancel a quote after it is created?

Quotes cannot be canceled after they are created. If you decide not to proceed, tell the sales representative who contacts you within 48 business hours.

### What should I do if I no longer want a quote?

If you no longer want the quote, select "End this quote" to exit the quoting flow.

### Can I change products, license quantities, or plans before confirming my quote?

Yes. Before confirming the quote and providing your email address, ask the agent to add or remove eligible products, change quantities, or update available plan and billing options. After the quote is created, request changes from a sales representative or create a new quote.

## Quote delivery and sharing

### Can I receive my quote in chat instead of sharing my email address?

Your email address is used to send you a shareable copy and enable our sales representative to support you throughout the process. The email will also include next steps and your quote ID.

### Can I share the quote with my manager or finance team?

Yes. You can forward the quote email to your manager or finance team for internal review and approval.

### What is the difference between a subscription term and billing frequency?

The subscription term is the length of your commitment, such as one month or one year. Billing frequency is how often you pay. For example, an annual subscription billed monthly still has an annual commitment. Available combinations vary by offer.

## Payment and billing

### Does creating a quote place an order or charge my credit card?

No. Creating or receiving a quote does not place an order or charge your card. To purchase, use the checkout link, sign in or sign up, review the proposal and billing details, accept the terms, and place the order.

### Can I pay by debit card, invoice, or wire transfer instead of credit card?

Credit cards are currently the only payment method supported through this quoting experience. Contact a seller for help purchasing by debit card, invoice, or wire transfer.

### Can I change the billing frequency from annual to monthly while creating a quote?

Yes. You can switch between annual and monthly billing before submitting your email address.

### Can I change an existing quote from annual to monthly billing?

After a quote is created, billing options cannot be changed in chat. A sales representative will follow up and can help update the billing options.

### Does my quote add products to the regular Microsoft website cart?

No. The quote-specific link opens a separate proposal checkout rather than the regular Microsoft website cart. It is configured for the products and quantities in your quote. Review the proposal, billing details, and terms before placing the order.

### Do I have to wait until my renewal date to convert my existing licenses?

The quoting experience doesn't currently support changes or conversions to an existing subscription. A sales representative can help review your current subscription, renewal terms, and available licensing options.

### Why does the pricing page show a different price?

The quote reflects the same base pricing shown on the Microsoft website. If a web promotion is available, the promotional pricing will be applied to the final purchase link provided by the sales representative. Review the product and offer details to ensure you are comparing the same options. A sales representative can also help clarify any pricing differences.

### Why is the billing market field locked?

The quote is created for a specific market, and the market associated with that quote carries into the purchase experience. If the market on the quote is incorrect, you may need to create a new quote for the appropriate supported market.

### Why can't I select a different market once the quote is created?

The purchase experience is tied to the market where your quote was created. To purchase in another supported market, you may need to start a new quoting experience for the appropriate market rather than changing the market on the existing quote.

## Next steps after creating a quote

### What happens after I create a quote?

If no additional validation is needed, you'll receive a draft quote and a checkout link in chat and by email. Use the link to complete your purchase. A sales representative will also reach out to offer support.

If additional validation is needed, you'll receive a draft quote in chat and by email to review and share internally. A sales representative will contact you to request additional details, then email you the final quote and checkout link.

### Why might I need to wait for a sales representative instead of purchasing online immediately?

If you are eligible to purchase directly, you will receive a checkout link. Otherwise, a sales representative may need to confirm details such as products, quantities, and billing options. They will also verify that your account has the required prerequisite products before issuing the final checkout link.

### How soon will the sales team contact me after I create a quote?

A sales representative should follow up within 48 business hours.

### Can a sales representative call me about my quote instead of emailing me?

A sales representative will contact you by email first. If you prefer a phone call, reply to the email and request one.

### How long is my quote valid?

Quotes expire 60 days after they are created. If you need more time, create a new quote when you are ready.

### What information will the sales representative receive?

The representative will receive the information you entered during the quoting flow, such as selected products and quantities, so they can help with next steps and answer questions.

## Troubleshooting and common questions

### I did not receive the draft quote by email. Where can I find it?

After your quote is created, a copy is sent to the email address you provided. Check your inbox and spam or junk folder. If you still cannot find it, type "chat with sales" to connect with a sales representative, who can resend the quote or help you create a new one.

### What should I do if I entered the wrong email address when creating a quote?

You can restart the quoting flow to create a new quote or type "chat with sales" to connect with the sales team for help creating one.

### Why can I no longer see the quote in chat?

For privacy, quotes are not available in chat after the session ends. A copy is sent to the email address you provided.

### What should I do if my checkout link does not work or my quote needs additional review?

Follow the instructions in the quote email and contact your sales representative when they reach out to you. Some quotes require validation of products, quantities, billing details, or prerequisite licenses. If you have not completed the purchase, a representative will follow up within 48 business hours. If the quote has expired, create a new one.
