# Usage-Based Billing FAQ for Microsoft 365 Copilot Business

This FAQ helps you understand usage-based billing, Copilot credits.

## Does a Microsoft 365 Copilot Business license include unlimited AI usage?

Your Microsoft 365 Copilot Business license includes core Copilot capabilities, such as chat and Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps. Certain advanced experiences, including Copilot Cowork and other long-running, autonomous agentic capabilities, use Copilot credits and are billed based on actual usage.

## What Microsoft Copilot features are usage-based?

Everyday AI experiences, such as chat and Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps, are included with your Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Advanced experiences use Copilot credits and are billed based on actual usage. Current examples include Copilot Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and the Work IQ APIs. Additional usage-based services may be added over time.

## How does usage-based billing work?

Usage-based billing lets you pay for eligible advanced AI experiences based on actual usage. These experiences consume Copilot credits, while the Copilot capabilities included with your subscription remain included. [Learn more about usage-based billing and Copilot Credits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/usage-based-billing-overview-copilot-credits).

## What is needed to set up usage-based billing?

Admins can set up usage-based billing through [Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/copilot/costmanagement/overview). For eligible new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business purchases, pay-as-you-go usage-based billing may instead be configured by default. In that case, Microsoft creates the required Azure resources and applies a spending policy with default limits, reducing the billing setup needed to access eligible usage-based experiences.

## Are customers automatically charged when usage-based billing is enabled?

No. Enabling usage-based billing does not by itself create charges. Customers are charged only when they use eligible metered services.

## What spending controls are created?

The default spending policy limits usage to 4,000 Copilot credits per user per month, with charges based on actual usage, and applies to future eligible usage-based experiences. If the organization does not already have the required pay-as-you-go Azure resource or spending policy, Microsoft creates them during the eligible purchase and product setup.

## Can customers modify the spending limit?

Yes. Admins can review and adjust spending policies and usage controls in [Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/copilot/costmanagement/overview) to align with organizational needs.

## Can customers turn off usage-based billing?

Yes. Admins can disable usage-based billing through Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center. Disabling it removes access to experiences that require usage-based billing; capabilities included with the subscription remain available.

## Where do customers manage spending and billing policies?

Admins can review and manage usage controls, spending policies, consumption, and costs through [Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/copilot/costmanagement/overview).

## Which services use Copilot credits?

Current eligible usage-based experiences that use Copilot credits include Copilot Cowork, Code, Autopilot, the Work IQ APIs, and other long-running, autonomous agentic capabilities.

## How can customers view usage?

Admins can monitor consumption and costs and manage usage controls and spending policies through [Cost Management in the Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/copilot/costmanagement/overview).

## What happens when a customer reaches the spending limit?

When a user reaches the admin-established spending limit, they will see a message explaining that they have reached the limit and can request more capacity from their administrator. The admin can then adjust the spending policy or usage controls to align with the organization's needs.
