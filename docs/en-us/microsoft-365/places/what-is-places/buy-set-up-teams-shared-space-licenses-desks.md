# Buy and set up Microsoft Teams Shared Space licenses for desks

## How the licensing model works

Desk licensing for Microsoft Places is delivered through the existing shared space licensing model. You purchase one paid Microsoft Teams Shared Space \(MTSS\) license, and that purchase entitles you to three Microsoft Teams Shared Space Single Space \(MTSSS\) licenses priced at zero.

The entitlement ratio is fixed at one paid license to three single-space licenses. If you need coverage for four thousand desks, you purchase one thousand MTSS licenses and then acquire three thousand MTSSS licenses against them.

| License | Price | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft Teams Shared Space \(MTSS\) | $8 per desk per month\* | The paid base license. Buy one for every three single-space licenses you intend to acquire. |
| Microsoft Teams Shared Space Single Space \(MTSSS\) | $0 | A free sub-SKU, three per paid license. Assignable only to desks; Teams devices will not accept it. |
| Effective desk rate | About $2 per desk per month\* |  |

Important

License costs shown do not include applicable taxes, fees, or other charges, which may vary by region and billing terms.

## Before you begin

Confirm all of the following before you place an order.

- Know which agreement your organization transacts on. The purchase path differs between a Microsoft Customer Agreement \(MCA\) and an Enterprise Agreement \(EA\), and the two portals behave differently.
- Billing account administrator or billing profile contributor permissions. Without them the Marketplace section of the Microsoft 365 admin center will not appear at all, and no order can be placed.
- A configured billing account and billing profile. On the MCA path the single-space SKU is only visible once an MCA billing account exists, and an order is blocked until a billing profile is attached.
- At least one paid Shared Space license already in the tenant. The single-space SKU does not become selectable until the base license is present.
- License assignment rights in the Microsoft 365 admin center, and a published Places building with the desks you intend to license.

## The process at a glance

The four-step process is the same regardless of which portal you use. The sections that follow provide portal-specific instructions for steps 1 and 3.

Step 1. Acquire a paid Microsoft Teams Shared Space \(MTSS\) license.

Step 2. Assign that license to a desk.

Step 3. Acquire three Microsoft Teams Shared Space Single Space \(MTSS-SS\) licenses for every paid license you own for no additional cost.

Step 4. Assign each single-space license to one of three further desks.

Important

The three single-space licenses are not generated or provisioned automatically. Purchasing the paid license creates the entitlement; you must then actively acquire the single-space licenses yourself. Customers who purchase the base license and wait for the free licenses to appear will wait indefinitely.

## Purchasing through the Microsoft 365 admin center \(MCA\)

This purchase path applies to organizations with a Microsoft Customer Agreement \(MCA\). Both licenses are available from the **Microsoft Teams Shared Space** listing in **Marketplace**. On the product page, use the **Select a plan** menu to choose the paid or no-cost plan, and submit a separate order for each plan. Assign each license to an individual desk account from **Users** in the Microsoft 365 admin center.

### A worked example

An organization needs 6,000 reservable desks. It buys 1,500 licenses on the Microsoft Teams Shared Space plan. That entitles it to 1,500 multiplied by three, or 4,500, licenses on the Microsoft Teams Shared Space - Single plan, which is free at every subscription length. The steps below follow these numbers.

### Step 1. Buy the paid Shared Space \(MTSS\) licenses

- In the Microsoft 365 admin center, select **Marketplace** in the left navigation.
- Under **Billing account** view, check the account named next to Products available for and confirm it is marked \(MCA\). If it is not the account you intend to buy on, select **Change billing account** before going any further.
- In the search box above the product list, type **Shared Space**. A single result returns, titled Microsoft Teams Shared Space.
- On that product card, select **Details**. This opens the Product details page, which is where both plans are bought.
- Under **Build your plan**, open the **Select a plan dropdown** and choose **Microsoft Teams Shared Space**. This is the **paid** plan. **The Plans and pricing tab** further down the page lists both plans side by side if you want to confirm the prices first.
- Choose a **Subscription length** of 1 month, 1 year, or 3 years.
- Choose a **Billing frequency of Pay** monthly, Pay yearly, or Pay every 3 years.
- In Select license **quantity**, enter the number of paid licenses you need. In the example, that is 1,500.
- Review the **Cost** panel on the right of the page, then select **Buy**. Final price, taxes and fees are shown at checkout before the purchase completes.

### Step 2. Assign the paid licenses to desk accounts

- In the left navigation, expand **Users** and select **Active users**.
- Search for the desk account you want to license and select it to open its details.
- Choose **Assign licenses**, select **Microsoft Teams Shared Space**, and **save**.
- Repeat for each desk you are covering with a paid license. One license covers one desk; in the example, 1,500 desks.

### Step 3. Buy the free single-space \(MTSS-SS\) licenses

- Return to **Marketplace** and open the same **Microsoft Teams Shared Space listing**, then select **Details** again. There is no separate listing to find.
- Under **Build your plan**, open the **Select a plan** dropdown and choose **Microsoft Teams Shared Space - Single**. This is the free plan.
- Choose a **Subscription** length. The Single plan is listed as free for 1 month, free for 1 year, and free for 3 years, so the term affects only the commitment period, not the price.
- Choose a **Billing frequency** to match.
- In **Select license quantity**, enter the full number you are entitled to, which is three for every paid license. In the example, that is 4,500. Enter it as one quantity rather than placing repeated orders of three.
- Confirm the Cost panel reads zero, then select Buy.

### Step 4. Assign the single-space licenses to the remaining desk accounts

- In the left navigation, expand **Users** and select **Active users**.
- Search for the next desk account and select it to open its details.
- Choose Assign licenses, select **Microsoft Teams Shared Space - Single**, and **save**.
- Repeat for each remaining desk. In the example, the 4,500 single licenses cover 4,500 desks, which with the 1,500 from step two gives the full 6,000.

## Purchasing through Volume Licensing \(Enterprise Agreement\)

If your organization has an Enterprise Agreement \(EA\), use the **Volume Licensing Center** instead of the **Marketplace** purchase flow. In the Volume Licensing Center, reserve the paid Microsoft Teams Shared Space licenses and request the no-cost Microsoft Teams Shared Space Single Space licenses separately. The same one-to-three entitlement applies: for each paid license, you can request three single-space licenses.

### Step 1. Acquire the paid license

- Acquire the paid Microsoft Teams Shared Space license through your reseller by using a purchase order. If the SKU is already included in your Enterprise Agreement, you can instead reserve the required quantity in the Volume Licensing Center. Reserved licenses are charged at your next true-up.

Important

You can reserve a SKU only if it is already included in your Enterprise Agreement. If the SKU is not available, contact your reseller to place the initial order.

### Step 2. Acquire the free single-space licenses

- In the Volume Licensing Center, search for **Microsoft Teams Shared Space** and select the SKU. The single-space option becomes selectable only after a paid license exists on the agreement; if you look before that, the option is absent and there is nothing to request.
- Select **Add usage** on the single-space SKU and enter the quantity you are entitled to, which is three for every paid license you hold.
- Confirm the **quantity**, choose **Reserve services**, and accept the terms presented. No price is shown because the SKU carries none.
- The request enters a pending state and provisions shortly afterwards. Repeat the request as you add paid licenses.

### Step 3. Assigning licenses to desks

Both plans are assigned the same way, and each desk needs exactly one license to unlock three capabilities: reservation, auto-release/check-in, and analytics. For both paid and single-space licenses, follow the assignment instructions above in [Step 4.](#step-4-assign-the-single-space-licenses-to-the-remaining-desk-accounts)

## Best practices

### Plan lead time into your rollout.

Plan for procurement lead time before assigning licenses. Orders placed through a reseller can take several weeks to complete, so obtain licenses before bringing a new building or floor online. Maintain a small reserve of licenses to avoid delays when additional desks become available.

## Follow the entitlement and assignment requirements for desk licenses

Desk entitlements are based on a one-to-three licensing ratio. For every qualifying paid Shared Space license, up to three single-space desk licenses may be used for eligible desk scenarios. Plan and assign licenses according to this entitlement so your deployment remains aligned with the licensing terms. Single-space desk licenses are intended only for desks and should not be assigned to Teams devices such as panels or common area phones.
