# Collaborate and share securely with Microsoft 365 Business Premium

[![Diagram with Collaborate And Share Securely highlighted.](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-premium/media/mission5.png?view=o365-worldwide)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-premium/media/mission5.png?view=o365-worldwide#lightbox)

The best way to collaborate and share securely is to use Microsoft Teams, and Teams is included in Microsoft 365 Business Premium. With Teams, all your files and communications are in a protected environment. With Teams, you can help set up and configure secure file sharing and communication within your organization.

Your steps are to:

- [Create Teams for collaboration](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-premium/create-teams-for-collaboration?view=o365-worldwide).
- [Set up meetings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-premium/set-up-meetings?view=o365-worldwide).
- [Share files and videos](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-premium/share-files-and-videos?view=o365-worldwide).
- [Create a communication site](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business-premium/create-communications-site?view=o365-worldwide).

Once you've achieved these steps, proceed to [Set up and secure managed devices](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/security-and-compliance/m365bp-devices-enrollment).
